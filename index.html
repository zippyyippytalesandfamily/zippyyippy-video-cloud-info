App information

A private workspace for our video stories.

ZippyYippy Video Cloud is an owner-operated production tool for preparing, rendering and reviewing ZippyYippy Tales videos using Google Drive and GitHub.

This is the public information page, not the production workspace. Private videos, character references, credentials and review links are not published here.

How the workspace is intended to work

The owner provides a topic, reviews a screenplay and storyboard, and approves a defined budget before paid generation. Cloud processing returns a private video for the owner's review. Automated topic-to-video production is still being qualified.

Why it connects to Google Drive

The app uses selected reference files and stores private production outputs. It requests the limited drive.file permission: files created by the app or explicitly opened or shared with it, rather than unrestricted access to the whole Drive.

Owner control

Google authorization can be revoked through the owner's Google Account. Paid generation requires separate owner approval. See the privacy notice for data handling and deletion details.
